# 奇症略述(1868;Bodleian Sinica 2021) / Qi zheng lue shu [Brief account of remarkable cases]: Transcription dataset

## 原書內容與用途 / Historical content

《奇症略述》（Qi zheng lue shu）是館目歸於 John Glasgow Kerr（J. G. Kerr，嘉約翰）的中文醫館報告，由博濟醫局在廣州（Canton）刊行；Sinica 2021 為同治七年（1868）本。原書以病例敘事與病者圖像文字呈現 Medical Missionary Society 廣州醫院的活動，可供研究晚清醫館的自我呈現、慈善醫療、病例敘述與醫學圖像。v0.2.1 依 Bodleian Sinica 2021 的 19 幅影像轉錄 31 個目標物理面，提供 TXT、JSON、TEI XML 與官方影像來源對應。文字為 AI 暫定轉錄，未經人類全文雙校或專家審定。

Qi zheng lue shu (奇症略述) is a Chinese hospital-report publication attributed in the Bodleian catalogue to John Glasgow Kerr (J. G. Kerr) and published by 博濟醫局 (Boji Medical Institution) at Canton (Guangzhou); Sinica 2021 is the 1868 witness. Through case narratives and illustrated accounts of patients, it presents the work of the Medical Missionary Society’s Hospital at Canton, offering material for studying institutional self-presentation, medical charity, case narrative and medical illustration in late Qing China. Version 0.2.1 transcribes 31 target physical faces across 19 images of Bodleian Sinica 2021, providing TXT, JSON and TEI XML with references to the official source images. The text is a provisional AI transcription without human double collation or expert review.
